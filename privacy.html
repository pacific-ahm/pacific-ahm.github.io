Privacy policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Synthexis is a desktop application for managing research papers. It runs on your computer and keeps your library (papers, notes, highlights and settings) in a folder on your computer. The developer runs no servers and collects no data about you or your use of the app.

Google Drive

If you choose to sync with Google Drive, the app asks Google for access to your Drive with the drive.file permission. This lets the app see and change only the files and folders it creates itself; it cannot see any other file in your Drive.

  • The app uses this access only to store a copy of your library in your Drive and to keep your computers in step with it.
  • Your sign-in is stored on your computer, encrypted with Windows' own protection, and is never sent to the developer or anyone else.
  • Data received from Google APIs is not shared with anyone, not used for advertising, and not used to train AI models.
  • You can stop at any time by removing Google Drive from sync in the app, or by removing the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Synthexis's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Online services the app contacts

To look up details of papers and find free copies, the app sends identifiers such as DOIs, arXiv IDs and paper titles (never your notes or files) to public scholarly services: OpenAlex, Crossref, DataCite, arXiv, Semantic Scholar, Europe PMC, PubMed Central, Open Library and Google Books.

If you turn on the AI features and enter your own API key, the text you ask about (for example a paper's text) is sent to the provider you chose (Anthropic or Google Gemini) under that provider's terms. With Ollama, it stays on your computer.
