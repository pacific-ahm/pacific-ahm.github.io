Synthexis

Read deeply, synthesise clearly.

A desktop app for researchers that keeps your papers, highlights, notes and citations together, and helps you turn many papers into one clear understanding.

Collect

Add papers by DOI, arXiv ID, PDF or from your browser. Details are filled in from public scholarly sources, and free PDFs are found for you.

Read

A first pass shows a paper's abstract, outline, figures, key numbers and conclusion at a glance. Highlight, comment and draw on the PDF.

Synthesise

Citation maps, survey tables and summary sheets help you see how papers relate and what they add up to.

Cite

Cite while you write in Word, in APA, IEEE, Vancouver or any other CSL citation style, with a bibliography that keeps itself up to date.

Your library stays yours

Synthexis keeps your library on your own computer. If you choose, it can keep your computers in step through a folder, your local network or your own Google Drive. There are no Synthexis servers, and nothing about you is collected.
